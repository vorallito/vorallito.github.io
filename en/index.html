Who checked

I use AI in my work, which is precisely why I verify before I recommend.

On any given day, I am orchestrating several Codex and Claude Code sessions on a single project, each of which has to know what the others have decided. What I have come to understand is that the real danger is not that AI gets things wrong. It is that it gets them wrong with complete confidence, and nobody notices.

So a single principle governs everything I do: whatever I have not verified carries a “not verified” stamp. I keep a shared log that every session consults before it begins and updates before it signs off. No figure makes it in without a source.

This pharmacy abides by the same principle.

Before you open the box

Read the label

The rating is mine, based on fixed criteria. It measures relative risk. It is not a security audit.

Over the counter

Published by Anthropic or by the company that owns the tool, and updated in the last month. Lowest relative risk. Read the SKILL.md anyway.

Prescription only

Community project with more than a thousand stars, updated in the last six months. Test it on a real case, with no sensitive data, before bringing it to your team.

Experimental sample

New, small, not actively maintained, or very specific. Use it as a reference. Keep it out of critical processes.

Compounded formula

There is no ready-made version. I wrote the recipe, and you build the skill with Skill Creator the way you need it.

How to install in two minutes

  1. Turn on the feature. In Settings › Capabilities, enable “Code execution and file creation”. On Team and Enterprise plans, an admin turns it on.
  2. Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF come ready on claude.ai. No install needed.
  3. Skills from the official directory: Customize › Skills › “+” › Browse skills.
  4. Third-party skill: download the skill folder, zip it and upload it in Customize › Skills › “+” › Upload.
  5. Official plugins (paid plans) appear in Customize › Plugins. They were built for Cowork and also run in Claude Code.
Checked in the Anthropic help center on Sep 25, 2026. Menus change. Some skills need a terminal; the card says so.

Your data, your responsibility

  • Company account for company data. Personal and business plans have different data-use rules.
  • Your data-protection law applies (GDPR in the EU, LGPD in Brazil, and similar laws elsewhere). Processing may happen outside your country. Health, religion, salary and performance-review data are sensitive or need extra care.
  • Ask where the data goes. Some skills send content to third-party services. The cards say when that happens.
  • Emails and PDFs can carry hidden instructions. With connectors on, review what Claude did before you approve anything it sends.
  • Legal and accounting playbooks in the official bundles follow US rules. Adapt them to your jurisdiction before use.

How this pharmacy was put together

Criteria, limits and transparency

  • What I checked. On Sep 25, 2026, every link opened, every repository had its documentation read and its last-update date checked. Checking is not testing: I did not run any skill on a real task for this edition.
  • Who it is for. About three in four pills serve people who work outside engineering. The tech and design ones are grouped together at the end.
  • What was left out. Projects that were removed, archived or idle for months, and clones that inflate their own install count. When a project moved, the link points to the new address.
  • One pill, many skills. Bundles and plugins group dozens of skills and count as one pill.
  • “Trending” marks community projects with more than 40,000 GitHub stars on the check date. Popularity is not a guarantee of quality.
  • Gaps. I found nothing reliable for procurement, the public sector, healthcare, non-software engineering, corporate training and personal life. For personal life, I left formulas so you can build your own.
  • Independence. A Vorallito guide, with no ties to Anthropic or to the authors listed, and no sponsored links.
  • Privacy. This page does not ask for your email. Favorite stars stay in your browser only.
  • Metaphor. Pharmacy, leaflet and label are editorial devices. No pill here is a medicine or a clinical tool.
